HealthBridge

Helps you use this page. It changes only what you see.

Welcome to HealthBridge

  • Read a page in a simpler way, with hard words explained and read aloud.
  • Fill in a form one question at a time.
  • Check a site for problems, and tell the clinic about them.
  • Change colors, spacing and sizes, just for you.

Your information stays on this computer. HealthBridge never sends what is on the page anywhere, and it never sends a form unless you choose Send.

It only works on a page after you press the HealthBridge button, and it keeps form answers in memory for up to an hour so it can put them back if a site signs you out.
